Dr. Adewoyin M. Ogunmolasuyi
PhD · Founder & CEO, M-Quadruple Biotek Ltd
Unveiling Biosensing & Diagnostics

In a Pioneering Lab

Dr Adewoyin M. Ogunmolasuyi is an emerging African researcher with global ambitions to deliver innovations in medical diagnostics, using novel biomedical engineering, including expertise in microfluidics and biosensing.

During his doctoral studies, he constructed a microfluidic paper analytical device (µPAD) of similar geometry to lateral flow device to deliver an aptamer-tethered enzyme capture (APTEC) and enzyme-linked oligonucleotide assays for detection of Plasmodium falciparum lactate dehydrogenase in malaria.

  • Employed several electrochemical biosensor designs including electrochemical impedance spectroscopy (EIS), cyclic voltammetry (CV), chronoamperometry
  • Awarded research grant internationally by USAID through African Research Network for Neglected Tropical Diseases (ARNTD) for low-cost diagnostic tests
  • Developed international consortium with University of Glasgow and Rhodes University with networks of collaborators in Nigeria

What we do

Here are some of the areas we excel in the most

Biosensor Training

Our training programs focus on the fabrication of biosensors, specializing in colorimetric, electrochemical, and molecular diagnostics to empower the next generation of diagnostic professionals.

Pathogen Analysis

We provide in-depth molecular and microbiology analysis of pathogens through advanced assays such as polymerase chain reaction (PCR), Loop mediated sequence amplification (LAMP), and Recombinase polymerase amplification (RPA).

Microfluidic Diagnostics

We specialize in creating cutting-edge point-of-care diagnostic devices for infectious diseases, utilizing advanced microfluidic technology to ensure rapid and accurate results.

Aptamer Research

Our team conducts pioneering research in aptamers using SELEX (systematic evolution of ligands by exponential enrichment) technology, advancing both therapeutic and diagnostic applications.

Diagnostic Solutions

We offer expert technical assistance and cutting-edge technology to diagnostics companies, enhancing their capabilities and ensuring the highest standards of diagnostic accuracy.

General Merchandising

Our store offers quality research merchandise for all your scientific needs. From lab equipment to specialized tools, we cater for researchers' requirements.
